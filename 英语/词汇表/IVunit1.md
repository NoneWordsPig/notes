# IV Unit 1 Vocabulary

## Reading 1 · New words

**commence**
*v.*  开始
>commence speech: the speech at the graduation ceremony

**commencement**
*n.*
1. （大学或高中的）毕业典礼
- e.g. The high school commencement speaker was giving a rather inspiring address.
2. 开始；开端
- e.g. Would passengers please turn off their mobile phones before the commencement of the flight?

**faculty**
*n.*
1. *(AmE)* （大学的）全体教员
- e.g. A drop in enrollment (招生人数) will affect students, faculty, and administrators.
2. 系；部；院
- e.g. These plans were part of a reorganization that divided the university into six faculties.

**confront**
*vt.*
1. 处理、解决（问题或困境）
- e.g. You must confront your fears and doubts and take risks again and again.
2. （问题或困境）临到某人；使某人面对问题或困境
- e.g. Here are some problems that have confronted scientists all over the world in the past decade.

**quitter**
*n. [C]* — （口语）遇到困难就放弃的人；半途而废的人
- e.g. I have asked you to do this tough task because I know you are not a quitter.

**mediocre**
*a.* — 不太好的
- e.g. Tourists crowd the gift shops along the street to buy mediocre products at high prices.

**slipshod**
*a.* — 马虎的，随便的，草率的（含贬义）
- e.g. She complained that the research project was delayed mainly due to slipshod management.

**forgiving**
*a.* — 宽容的；宽大的
- e.g. The public was more forgiving of the president's mistakes than the press and fellow politicians.

**deadline**
*n. [C]* — 截止时间；最后期限
- e.g. Without extra help, it's going to be very difficult for us to meet the Friday deadline.

**garrulous**
*a.* — （尤指对琐事）喋喋不休的；饶舌的
- e.g. I had talked too much about myself and felt like a garrulous old fool.

**toss**
*vt.* — 扔，掷，抛（尤其指轻的东西）
- e.g. She picked up the package and casually tossed it into her handbag.

**dull**
*a.*
1. 迟钝的；愚笨的
- e.g. It's such a simple question. If you don't understand then you are duller than I thought.
2. 无趣味的；不刺激的；不生动的
- e.g. The documentary lasts for more than two-and-a-half hours, and there is scarcely a dull minute.

**unimaginative**
*a.* — 无创意的；缺乏想象力的
- e.g. He simply couldn't understand why she thought him to be a stiff and unimaginative person.

**fantasy**
*n. [C, U]* — 幻想；想象
- e.g. Young children sometimes cannot distinguish between fantasy and reality.

**pretence**
*n. [U, sing.]* *(BrE)* — 假装；作假；做作
- e.g. Now that the pretence was over, he could tell them what he really thought.

**conventional**
*a.* — 依照传统的；符合习俗的
- e.g. It's not a hotel, in the conventional sense, but rather a whole village turned into a hotel.

**spoil**
*vt.* *(spoiled, spoiled; BrE also spoilt, spoilt)* — 使损坏；糟蹋；破坏
- e.g. They had an entire week, so there was no need to rush into a conversation that might spoil everything.

**deprive**
*vt.* `~ sb. of sth.` — 剥夺
- e.g. Some people worry that the advances in information technology may deprive citizens of their privacy.

**adequate**
*a.* — 足够的；充分的；合乎需要的
- e.g. The lunchtime menu is more than adequate to satisfy the appetite.

**attentive**
*a.*
1. 关心的；善帮忙的
- e.g. It is an excellent childcare center for babies as the staff are friendly, relaxed and attentive.
2. 注意的；留心的；专心的
- e.g. People who watch the news tend to be more attentive to what is on the screen.

**co-worker**
*n. [C]* — 同事；同僚
- e.g. The manual explains what to do if a co-worker is injured and in need of medical attention.

**insult**
*vt.* — 侮辱；冒犯
- e.g. In some cultures, you insult your host if you do not accept their offer of food.

**parlous**
*a.* *(fml.)* — 状况糟坏的；非常危险的
- e.g. Relations between the two countries have been in a parlous state for some time.

**tolerate**
*vt.*
1. 容忍；宽容
- e.g. According to the spokesperson, the government is not prepared to tolerate the situation any longer.
2. 忍受；忍耐
- e.g. For years the workers have had to tolerate low wages and terrible working conditions.

**adolescent**
*n. [C]* — 青少年（常指 12 至 18 岁之间的人）
- e.g. We know that adolescents are neither children nor adults and they have specific developmental needs.

**unlearn**
*vt.* *(口语)* — 设法忘记，抛弃（已学会的东西）
- e.g. You must start by unlearning all the bad habits your previous piano teacher taught you!

**preparatory**
*a.* — 预备的；准备的
- e.g. Months of preparatory work needs to be done before construction work can really begin.

**formulate**
*vt.*
1. 仔细思考并明确表达
- e.g. She has lots of good ideas, but she has difficulty formulating them in front of investors.
2. 规划；制定；准备
- e.g. The city government is currently formulating policies on road expansion to solve traffic problems.

**tolerant**
*a.* — 容忍的
- e.g. My friends are more tolerant, more willing to accept me for what I am.

**broaden**
*vt.* — 增长（知识、经验等）；扩大（活动范围等）
- e.g. The course helps school-leavers broaden their knowledge of the world of work.

**supposedly**
*ad.* — 据说；据称；一般相信；一般看来
- e.g. He was supposedly delivering some papers to a hotel but I think it was just an excuse to see her.

**erase**
*vt.*
1. 清除；消除；消灭
- e.g. Mr. Woods wants a convincing victory to erase doubts about his team's ability.
2. 删除（计算机文件）；抹去（磁带录音）
- e.g. It appears the names were accidentally erased from the computer hard drive.

**semester**
*n. [C]* — （尤其美国中学和大学的）一学期，半学年
- e.g. I was finishing my last semester in college and trying to look for a job in my hometown.

**midterm**
*n.*
1. *[C] (AmE)* 期中考试
2. *[U]* （政府任期的）中期

**final**
*n. [C]*
1. *(AmE)* （高中或大学的）期末考试
2. 决赛
- e.g. He's through to the men's tennis final for the first time in his career.

**due**
*a.* *(not before noun)* — 预定的；预期发生（到达）的
- e.g. They suddenly gave up the deal the day before they were due to sign the contract.

### Phrases and expressions

**drop out**
— 退学；辍学
- e.g. To everyone's surprise, Bill dropped out of college after his first year and joined a rock-and-roll band.

**look back**
— 回忆起；回顾
- e.g. When you look back on this experience in a year or two, it won't seem so bad.

**the easy way out**
— 省事的做法
- e.g. It is the easy way out to blame others for our failure though it doesn't make any sense.

**act out**
— 用行动来表现情绪（尤其愤怒或紧张）
- e.g. These teenagers are likely to act out their feelings of insecurity by being moody and aggressive.

**be rid of**
— 摆脱某人／某事物
- e.g. He found his job tedious and time-consuming and he was glad to be rid of it.

**pop into one's mind / head**
— 突然冒出（想到）
- e.g. List the product's strengths as well as weaknesses at random as they pop into your mind.

**get away with**
— （做错事）不被发现，不受惩罚
- e.g. It's time they put a stop to his bad behavior. He's been getting away with it for too long.

**get down to**
— 开始认真对待；开始努力做
- e.g. That's a tourist photo! I must get down to booking the hotel as soon as possible.

**by no means**
— 绝不；一点都不
- e.g. It is by no means clear where the money is going to come from to fund this project.

**stun**

**proceed**

**arch**

**eyebrow**

**shrug**

**slum**

**smug**

**leer**

**warehouse**

**industrial**

**pointedly**

**groan**

**audible**

**interrupt**

**slam dunk**

**verbatim**

**rustle**

**frantic**

### Phrases and expressions

**cut one's losses**
— 及时罢手以减少损失
- e.g. Cut your losses with projects that are not going to work and do things that make you feel comfortable.

**pack up**
— （在离开前）收好物品
- e.g. We arrived just as the musicians were packing up their instruments.

**smile broadly**
— 咧嘴笑
- e.g. He tiptoed closer, smiling broadly, waiting for Suzanne to spot him.

**cut into**
— 削减
- e.g. Some business owners are cutting into their profits to help raise money for the foundation.

**better still**
— 更好的选择是
- e.g. I started to hunt for a cheap restaurant nearby or, better still, a snack shop.

**hand out**
— 分发；散发
- e.g. He was busy handing out leaflets on the musical to the audience.

**reason out**
— （通过推理）解释，解决
- e.g. I spent the entire night reasoning out the solution to the puzzle.

**think through**
— 认真地考虑（某事可能产生的结果）
- e.g. People need time to think through what the changes in taxation will mean for them.
