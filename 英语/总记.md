##### Three purposes in reading
**reading for comprehension**: to get information and ideas
**reading for language**: to accumulate language expressions
**reading for production**: to learn structures and skills

#### write to refute
a common structure: three-part organizational pattern
- The opponent's argument: state others' opinions
- Your position: make clear the nature of your disagreement with the argument
- Your refutation: present more recent evidence, and explain why your assumptions hold up.
