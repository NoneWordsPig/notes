**unlimited** vs **limitless**
unlimited 不限的，多实物
- unlimited data
- unlimited calls
- unlimited access
- unlimited refills
- unlimited attempts
- unlimited budgets

limitless 无限的
- limitless potential
- limitless possibilities
- limitless imagination
- limitless imagination
- limitless creativity
- limitless ambition
- liitless horizons

come
- come along 突然遇到某些人/事，后接宾语；出现（不接宾语）
- come across 浏览；偶遇
- come by 拜访；获得

tune 
- tune out 不听
- tune in 认真听
- tune up 调音

equ- 平等
- equal v.&adj. 相等，等同
- equality n.平等
- equinox 昼夜平分 （-nox，尼克斯）
- equivalent n.等同物
- equilibrium n.平衡

term
- in economic terms/ in terms of economy: from the perspective of economy
- in ... terms; in terms of ...: expressed as sth.
	- He referred to your work in terms of high praise/ in flattering terms
- be on good/ friendly/ intimate/ bad, eta. terms with ...
- be on equal terms
- come to terms with
